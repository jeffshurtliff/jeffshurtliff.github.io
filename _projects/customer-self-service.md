## Problem

Customers relied heavily on assisted channels to open support cases, limiting the value of the online support experience and reducing opportunities for knowledge-led self-service.

## Responsibility

I led the strategy, administration, architecture, and delivery of the organization's primary customer support portal, online community, and knowledge experience while directing a distributed team.

## Actions

- Expanded portal case-management capabilities and clarified the digital support journey.
- Improved knowledge discovery and connected support content more directly to customer tasks.
- Advanced proactive-support and self-service capabilities alongside operational reporting.
- Coordinated platform, process, content, and stakeholder work rather than treating adoption as a purely technical problem.

## Outcome

Online support-case submission increased from approximately 24% to over 70%, establishing a stronger digital foundation for customer service and knowledge-led support.
