## Problem

Multiple customer-facing applications operated separately on AWS, increasing platform cost and integration complexity while fragmenting customer data and experience.

## Responsibility

I jointly led the consolidation initiative as the owner of the Salesforce ecosystem and connected application portfolio, aligning architecture, platform teams, stakeholders, and delivery priorities.

## Actions

- Evaluated which capabilities belonged on the strategic Salesforce platform.
- Coordinated migration and integration work across customer applications and platform teams.
- Helped establish Salesforce as the primary system of record for the consolidated experience.
- Balanced cost reduction with customer continuity, maintainability, security, and roadmap needs.

## Outcome

The consolidation saved more than $150,000 annually while reducing integration complexity and strengthening Salesforce's role as the strategic customer platform.
